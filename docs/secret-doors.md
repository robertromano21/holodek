# Secret Passages and Ordinary Keys

New level geometry attempts to place a seeded total of two to six concealed
chambers. Existing wall mass is preferred; open areas can receive small sealed
stone vaults. Placement rejects occupied or protected cells and any enclosure
that would disconnect an existing route. A crowded or unusually small level
can have fewer doors when safe placement is impossible. Cached existing levels
are not silently rebuilt.

`search room`, `search walls` or `search secret doors` near a concealed panel
uses an engine d20 check: DC 5, an 80% chance per nearby undiscovered panel.
The radius is 2.5 tiles with clear access to its approach and compatible floor
height. Searching again is allowed; it consumes another turn. Discovery does
not open the panel. Far-away doors cannot be revealed by naming them.

Stand directly beside a panel and press Space to open it. An intentional push
can also find a not-yet-discovered panel. Text entry, pause and pending combat
actions do not trigger this shortcut. Its opaque voxel panel lifts over 700 ms;
the committed cell becomes a real open door in collision, sight and the map.
Discovery/opening state is stored with the room geometry, with nonce-protected
actions and protection against stale browser metadata. Hidden chambers are
optional and never substitute for boss keys or quest-critical routes.

Ordinary keys favor the room containing their lock or an actual open route to
an unvisited adjacent room. Missing exits are not paths. Previously established
return exits are not randomly locked. Boss-key quest staging remains separate.
