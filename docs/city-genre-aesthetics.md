# Genre-Coherent Cities And Characters

The new streamed, enterable city fabric uses the original CityKit and native genre designs. It does not replace the established CotG architecture with a generic skyscraper extrusion. Names, residents, quests and encounters remain authored world data. Art recipes are deterministic construction techniques, not prefab scenarios.

## Historical Planning References

- Roman-derived towns can have principal crossing axes and forum-centered civic complexes, but older streets can be inherited and irregular. See [Ostia's road network](https://www.ostiaantica.beniculturali.it/en/educational-panels/general-panels/decumanus-and-road-network/) and [central civic complex](https://ostiaantica.cultura.gov.it/en/educational-panels-and-maps/the-central-area-and-the-official-complexes/).
- The forum, law courts, workshops, homes, water collection and crowned drainage streets are connected functions, not scattered props. See the official [Pompeii street guide](https://pompeiisites.org/en/pompeii-map/analysis/along-the-streets-of-pompeii/).
- Greek-derived places use agoras, stoas, peristyles and temple precincts; orthogonal planning is one tradition, not a universal shape. See [Priene's UNESCO submission](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6348/) and the [Athenian Agora excavation project](https://www.ascsa.edu.gr/excavations/athenian-agora/about-the-excavations).
- Byzantine-derived places retain or infill earlier networks and use fortified terrain, courts, domes and service institutions. Reuse those techniques without importing Christianity into a fictional pantheon. See [Byzantine domestic archaeology](https://www.exploringbyzantium.gr/EKBMM/Page?id=36&lang=en&name=ypomeleti&sub=530) and the [Met's Byzantine survey](https://www.metmuseum.org/de/essays/byzantium-ca-330-1453).
- Medieval/Renaissance direction includes organic, concentric, planned market-grid and linear settlements, adapted to terrain, inherited parcels and local history. These are authoring directions; the existing bounded street graph and parcel fitter are not a new exact historical city simulator.
- Renaissance planning is not a requirement to erase medieval neighborhoods. [Pienza](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/789/) demonstrates piazza-centered civic renewal within inherited fabric; [Ferrara](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/733/) provides a reference for planned expansion and perspective. City authoring can choose civic renewal, an orthogonal extension, an axial connection or a radial fortress town, rather than using one template for all Renaissance places.
- [Palmanova's defenses](https://palmanova.it/en/palmanova_fortezza.php) use broad low embankments, angular bastions, curtains, moats and controlled entrances. Their artillery-era logic is conditional on the fictional world's established siege technology: architecture guidance does not silently introduce firearms, historical rulers or religions. Later ravelins and lunettes should not all be treated as original Renaissance construction.

## Implementation And Limits

`CityArchitectureStyles` supplies shared materials, native crown sources, genre lighting and optional historical traditions. Each new historic city's saved identity/history selects a stable tradition; no extra authoring request is added. Greek column/lintel idioms are not forced into Roman arcades. CitySites selects the existing native interior templates for the saved venue functions, restoring temples, basilicas and fortified civic buildings to the authored-location path.

An explicit Renaissance or Byzantine idiom takes precedence over incidental Classical vocabulary in the generated history; an already-saved tradition remains authoritative. The Renaissance expansion is authoring guidance consumed by the existing city identity and district requests, not a new physical star-fort generator. Exact radial bastion perimeters, city-wide axial sightline enforcement and trapezoidal piazza geometry still require dedicated layout work; no existing city is rewritten or advertised as having those features automatically.

Near voxel bodies and distant batched silhouettes use the same footprint and crown recipe. Full wall envelopes continue through occupied floors; the crown is additional height. Low-storey buildings receive roofs too. Existing floor addresses, room plans, interactions and LOD/cache bounds remain. Supported lobby arches use the original aqueduct arch geometry with a true open passage; sideways arches transpose the voxel geometry, not unsupported part rotations.

Fantasy-family homebrew rooms retain the native mounted torch rather than converting it into random rune/crystal fixtures. Common fantasy window panes and pre-electric venue lamps are warm. Special shrine/magic effects elsewhere remain available. The city windows still use the existing batched emissive/glow pass, not one dynamic light per pane.

Production world-roster prompts no longer include the same small futuristic example menus. Fantasy labels can recur; originality belongs in cultures, abilities, equipment and history. Model output still passes the existing validated roster contract and is saved per world. Existing rosters and saved city geometry are not rewritten. Mock/offline demonstration rosters remain seeded examples, never a production fallback: older drafts without rosters now explicitly request rebuilding instead of silently installing those examples. Player instructions are available before roster authoring. Ecology authoring receives the saved playable peoples and traditions so ordinary residents can belong to the same world.

This pass changes future construction and newly streamed parcels. Already-built saved buildings keep their geometry. A fresh world is needed to inspect new character rosters; this does not reroll an existing character or save.

## Classical Fantasy Construction Pass

CotG, fantasy and dark fantasy now share a character-scale height policy for new plans. The old upper-floor default was only 1.2 scene units, below the rendered character height of 1.8. New ordinary floors have at least 3 units of headroom; foodhalls retain 4.4 and temples 7.2. Floor-to-floor height and the exterior occupied envelope grow together. Modern, cyberpunk and science-fiction height policies are unchanged. Existing saved recipes are not rewritten or rebuilt.

New frontages reuse the original Doric/Ionic columns, entablatures and pediments around actual north/south street doors. Native procedural public interiors now use the original column and coffered-slab meshes, with bounded groin-vault or domed bays in sufficiently high, spacious rooms. Columns are accepted only if existing walkable routes stay connected. Vaults fit below the next floor, above character headroom, with structural ceiling coverage retained above them; stair shafts and their raised caps remain independent. This is a native assembly path, not a new LLM request or a full reconstruction of old authored temples. Existing authored layouts remain authoritative.

Occupied upper facades have seed-stable window bays with masonry jambs, sills, round-arched heads or lintels, projecting string courses and eaves cornices. Their bounded relief is lit in shared batches at both LODs; only warm panes use the existing emissive/glow pass. There is no per-window dynamic light. Near and far bodies still share the physical footprint/crown recipe. A browser refresh can dress an existing envelope visually, but new ceiling proportions and native interior assemblies require a newly constructed building/floor recipe.

References distinguish apartment blocks from elite houses rather than treating every Roman building as one type: the official Ostia guides document [commercial/residential use, covered access and tall windows](https://ostiaantica.cultura.gov.it/en/educational-panels-and-maps/larea-a-servizio-del-fiume/insula-del-soffitto-dipinto/) and [vestibules, stairs, columned halls and window-lit corridors](https://ostiaantica.cultura.gov.it/en/educational-panels-and-maps/i-quartieri-residenziali-della-classe-medio-alta/insula-delle-ierodule/). Courtyard/lightwell city blocks and a complete remapping of every original architectural template remain separate layout work; this pass does not pretend a painted recess is a usable courtyard.
