# Underworld Communities And Planes

## World Generation

New outdoor locations and vast caverns can receive optional LLM-authored villages. The model chooses whether a village belongs, its local history, pressures, population and two to four buildings. Existing inns, houses, temples, guild halls and citadels are joined by shade refuges, soul registries, watch houses and cinder forges. Entrances link to actual indoor rooms. Cavern settlements occupy a 192-cell-wide rock-roofed chamber, with smaller buildings beneath the ceiling.

Only unused, unreserved neighboring coordinates may become building entrances. Published routes, locks, quest placements, names and existing scenes are not overwritten. Villages are therefore an expansion for newly generated locations, not a destructive migration of a loaded save. `retort/villages.js` owns plan validation and publication; `retort/underworldLore.js` supplies shared lore and the new building catalog.

The shared lore prompt reinforces Children of the Grave and the underworld under siege, while allowing quiet places, humanoid residents and nonhumanoid souls. Frightened shades and demon guards are examples, not mandatory cast members. Outside protected starting/quest/service slots, the model chooses individual races, allegiances, personalities, routines and grouping. Village residents can work near a real building entrance or in the commons. The starting temple stays friendly and explicitly hostile temples retain their rules.

## Planar Portals

`retort/planarPortals.js` can author a rare purple-arch portal for a new location. Its destination is randomly selected at unused X, Y and Z coordinates in 100..1000, avoiding existing rooms and protected quest neighborhoods. Both directions store explicit destinations, so entering the portal again does not reroll it and the return arch leads home.

The model chooses the plane's terrain, palette, ecology, factions, knowledge of the war and optional local gods. Local gods are cosmology, not automatically spawned actors or replacements for the canonical pantheon. Native factions may be unaware, independent, allied or opposed. Planar identity persists through descriptions, population prompts, neighboring rooms and Retro Mode. Distance does not bypass Hades gates or advance quests. Local horizontal exploration stays within eight coordinates of the anchor and the 0..1000 coordinate bounds; the return portal remains available.

New music for portal thresholds and planar rooms requires an existing multi-movement SID suite form. The composer receives the saved realm's terrain, population and faction/god relationships, and chooses its own motifs, instruments, rhythms, contrast and return within the established arranger grammar. Unrelated realms are not forced into a Mortacia lament or war march. Ordinary room music and existing saved scores are not regenerated by this change; both SID archive variants continue through the standard music path.

Browser state synchronization cannot forge a portal or remove protected portal/village metadata. Existing ordinary navigation and rooms are retained.

## Supplemental Encounters

`assets/monsterRaces.js` shares the existing game's catalog between server and browser. Duplicate race names merge their descriptions; this is the existing fantasy catalog, not a new SRD import. Supplemental encounters use an exact enum of its 358 unique entries. Full creature descriptions accompany selection and character creation, including history, behavior, personality and voice. Descriptive powers do not grant unsupported combat mechanics.

`retort/randomEncounters.js` adds optional encounters without replacing regular residents or quest targets. The model may decline. Indoor rooms allow at most two single-creature additions; outdoors allow at most five groups of three. Minimum walking distances are 48/96 tiles and cooldowns 120/90 seconds respectively. Failed or declined attempts are capped too. The starting room, pauses, testing, active actions, ongoing discoveries and nearby existing residents prevent additions. Safe placement reuses the existing ahead-of-player encounter logic. Mechanical levels use the living non-Mortacia party, capped at 12, rather than importing unchecked catalog abilities.

Generation owns the server action lock and validates the active campaign, room, population revision and observer again before installing anything. Supplemental counters and individuals persist with the room population. No LLM fallback silently manufactures a cast.

## Recall And Exploration UI

MiniLM-assisted hybrid recall now also supplies atlas, regional adventure, settlement, encounter, quest and response planning. Planning has a bounded separate cache and a 1.5-second semantic wait budget with keyword fallback. Relevant witnessed history and committed outcomes are retained alongside current context. Recollections are evidence, never state updates or permission for NPC omniscience.

The level exploration map, separate from the combat map, displays discovered remaining objects with small game item icons and seen residents with portraits. Items disappear on pickup; characters remain at their last-seen coordinates rather than exposing offscreen motion. Hover names known icons, exits and mapped terrain without exposing undiscovered content.

## Menu Typography

`assets/gameUiFonts.css` uses locally bundled Press Start 2P in both Modern and Retro Mode. Console/log text is compact (6 CSS pixels, 1.85 line height) and stays white on black. Title-screen prose uses 15 pixels with 1.75 line height. `assets/gameUiPixels.css` adds stepped pixel borders and a pixel compass without filtering text or form controls. `assets/gameUiPixels.js` rasterizes the four shortcut icons to 24x24 once, retaining original SVGs as an error fallback. It does not run a per-frame UI filter or change gameplay, hit areas or sprites. Font licenses and source attribution are in `assets/fonts/`.

Party-icon names and HP use the same face at 6 pixels to fit their existing slots. Dice and the lower-left arithmetic/status feed retain their original compact monospace font. See [ROOM-ART.md](ROOM-ART.md) for per-room pixel illustrations and the shared, unlit artwork viewer.

The hamburger's Console / Log Font switch defaults to Retro and can restore the original 8-pixel monospace transcript face. It remembers the local preference without changing menus, headings, compass, artwork, graphics mode, black backgrounds or white text.

Restart the game server and reload the browser to activate new backend generation and shared assets. Implementation tests and isolated browser probes do not restart the running game or rewrite live saves.
