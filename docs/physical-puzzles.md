# Physical Room Puzzles

Newly populated rooms use one validated JSON mechanism design for the written puzzle, its exact solution, and the 3D apparatus. The initial toolbox contains procession buttons, three-position survey dials, and channel levers. A nearby readable plaque provides the visual clue; a cabinet visibly opens to reveal room-specific lore. The old independent ash/ember/bone seals and their popup controls are retired. Existing saved text puzzles and room geometry are not regenerated.

Hover a mechanism to see its name, crest, current setting, and a hand cursor when reachable. Decorative props also show hover names. Click to submit the ordinary text command through `chatbotprocessinput`. Its only routing change is complete-command travel detection: `west`, `w`, `go west`, `move west`, and `walk west` move; directions inside longer commands or dialogue do not. Text commands such as `press Sun button`, `turn West dial`, `set West dial to Moon`, `pull Crown lever`, and `read instruction plaque` operate the same mechanism. There is no puzzle menu. Click a nearby floor item, or drag it onto the backpack icon, to open the existing Take confirmation. Ordinary doors support mouse targeting through their existing command path.

Puzzle controls use dedicated upright voxel geometry: round engraved dial faces with state pointers, projecting/depressed buttons, hinged levers, readable plaques, and opening cabinet doors. Symbols come from each control's actual metadata, with deterministic glyphs for unfamiliar marks. Their small colored faces retain per-voxel detail instead of greedy merged colors. Existing saved fixtures derive the new appearance without changing geometry records, progress, or positions. New apparatus parts form a compact reachable group; the scenery and blueprint passes cannot produce misleading decorative copies of their controls.

The server checks the actor's actual room, life state, reach, unobstructed path, construction proof, and revision. It recomputes each control transition before committing it. Partial progress persists in the room record and dungeon IndexedDB cache. Wrong configurations reset or remain adjustable without exhaustion, consumable costs, random failure rolls, or a limit on attempts. First completion awards 250 XP to the acting character through the same character-sheet/state commit as other actions; repeat completions cannot reward again. Quest puzzle requirements use `puzzle_solved` with a bound room/mechanism ID; existing inventory requirements, boss keys, and locks remain authoritative.

Reading plaques, inspecting controls, changing settings, and rejected attempts are programmatic server actions with no LLM call. Mechanical physical commands return directly through the existing command route; room generation and completion narration keep their background task and polling behavior. Only a newly committed completion enters Retort for narration and quest follow-through. That pass cannot adjudicate the mechanism again, and a narration failure cannot undo its state or XP. Immediate action notices show control feedback, cabinet opening and awarded XP; the Game Log keeps the full detail. Separate notices also show confirmed pickups, committed adjudications, and combat deaths with the killer's name, without being filtered as character speech.

The legacy text-puzzle adjudicator still supports discovered items/exits and a one-time failed-attempt trap (1-7 damage per affected party member). Physical definitions currently guarantee XP and the opening lore cabinet; additional physical rewards or traps must be explicitly authored and validated before runtime, rather than invented by completion narration. While a server command is pending, command submission, world interactions and console links are guarded; movement and rendering are not paused, and local inventory confirmation sequences remain unchanged.

Construction checks every component, visual variant, and reachable operating position. Controls are placed after architecture and decoration, away from exits, items and reserved geometry. A new indoor layout can gain a small workshop recess in plain unreserved masonry if its corridors offer no safe site. An incomplete mechanism is rejected rather than becoming a narrated but unsolvable quest obstacle. Companions are prompted to offer observations only when asked for hints, never unsolicited solution sequences.

Logs: `[PuzzleDesign]`, `[PuzzleDesignBound]`, `[PuzzleConstruction]`, `[PuzzleAction]`, `[PuzzleResolution]`, `[AdjudicationApplied]`. Isolated Environment Lab exhibits `puzzle_buttons`, `puzzle_dials`, and `puzzle_channels` exercise the production geometry and mouse controls without modifying a campaign or calling an LLM.
