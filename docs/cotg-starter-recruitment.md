# Children of the Grave Starter Recruitment

New CotG games reserve one authored friendly resident to offer a local recruitment favor on approach or invitation. The model writes the title, dialogue, motives and conditions from engine-verified targets. The favor is offered, not automatically accepted. Completion and reporting use the normal mission system; a standing invitation recruits the same character after verified completion.

The existing observation, retrieval and defeat objectives remain available when their targets are present and eligible. An unfinished physical puzzle is also available, bound to its mechanism ID and verified completed state. All starter objectives remain in the opening room; no extra target, puzzle solution, commercial job or main-quest stage is fabricated. Ordinary starter shops and services stay disabled.

One roll per new starter population gives a 70% chance of one separate resident staying to tend and guard the temple entrance. The other 30% has no designated guardian. The role and roll are saved with the population, not rerolled on clicks or loads. At least two residents are generated so the guardian cannot consume the favor contact. Guardians retain their identity and can converse, but cannot be recruited. Other friendly residents retain normal consent-based recruitment.

Genre campaigns, established saved populations, ordinary recruitment decisions, encounter disposition and the CotG 65/35 authorship split are unchanged.

Invitation metadata is engine-bound, independent of whether a resident accepts or refuses. All live replies validate before social actions commit. In-world validation messages mentioning refusal are repairable; actual provider refusal remains a permanent error. SID status records bounded actionable synthesis errors separately from discarded speculative prefetch work.
