# Divine Encounters

The canonical pantheon comes from `assets/shrineIconography.js`, which indexes the campaign prompt. `pantheonCharacters.js` supplies encounter policies without inventing another pantheon.

- Mortacia retains her existing PC/party role. No second Mortacia is generated.
- Other gods have one identity per run in `0,0,0.divineCourt`, deterministic 300-500 maximum HP, strong combat statistics and independent motives. Evil alignments are hostile; other visiting gods are neutral, including good deities. Provoking a neutral can permanently change that relationship.
- First appearances request a bounded individual history, personality, voice, motivation and dialogue through the existing room-spawn LLM session, with the system prompt's pantheon canon and saved room facts. Validated narrative fields become the persistent character profile; stats, inventory, allegiance and mission proof are never taken from that response. Subsequent visits reuse the profile and recorded memories. A failed request retains the canonical fallback without blocking room entry.
- Ordinary resident and boss-name generation cannot impersonate a deity. Arithus (including the spelling Artihus) is reserved for the late-game, explicitly bound Throne Room of Hades. The final artifact/boss plan uses Sepulchra/Arithus rather than a random substitute.
- An occasional first-time populated non-start room receives a visitor. The third eligible population offers an early chance; later visits use a seeded one-in-six schedule. Regional occupations and shrine patrons influence selection. Previously encountered gods recur; visits do not reset HP, memory, inventory or death.
- A god occupies only one room. Moving a visitor retires the old encounter. A deity bound to an unresolved staged main quest remains at that room until resolution. A dead deity does not respawn.
- Divine side commissions place an actual, uniquely named document into an already populated room in the same region. A named mortal custodian receives the handoff and holds an explicit potion-payment escrow. Existing assistant succession handles a custodian's death. Dialogue, dropping the document or giving it to the wrong actor cannot complete the contract.
- Atinus can instead offer separate raids on existing temples of Arithus and Dantuea, to Mortacia, Suzerain or a mortal party. The exact room and its living, named hostile defenders are bound when the mission is seeded. Every bound defender must have recorded HP <= 0; an empty room, a narrated victory or one defender's death is insufficient. Each raid releases its potion escrow once, without changing main-quest progress or spawning its patron.
- Bound raid defenders remain at their temple and cannot be recruited while their obligation is open; combat movement and striking distance are unchanged. Suppression is recorded on the actual temple after verified completion. A returning patron can offer an existing commission from a different room, while its original custodian and escrow remain in place.
- Regions record faction allegiance, an evil patron for local hostiles and a shrine's patron. Hostile factions use Arithus, Dantuea, Sharlynn or Nameless Drake. Evil temples generate hostile priests and guards and never offer public shops or healing services. Neutral temples retain neutral healers. Patronage appears in the game console and the residents' narrative context; worship does not summon a god.
- Divine Audience uses the existing economy transaction endpoints. It offers real commissions and transfers only items actually in the visitor's personal inventory. Equipped items are not transferred. Recruitment is blocked in popups, typed commands, staged quest choices and state synchronization.
- Deities may serve as contacts in the existing main-quest scene compiler; these stages retain the original engine-verifiable objective and boss-key rules. Side commissions never advance the main quest or grant a boss key.

Set `DIVINE_ENCOUNTERS=off` to stop optional future visits. Existing saved identities and quest obligations remain intact. No running campaign is rewritten merely by deploying this code.
