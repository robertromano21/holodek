HOLODEK-1 SID SOUND EFFECTS + ROOM AMBIENCE (SFX pass, 2026-10-12)
====================================================================

WHAT IT IS
- 20 one-shot effects and 8 ambience loops, all recorded from a real SID (reSIDfp, 6581, PAL) on the build box.
  Each effect is a C64-style SFX driver: one 25-byte dump of the SID registers $D400-$D418 per 50 Hz frame,
  assembled with xa into a tiny PSID and recorded with sidplayfp. The designs use pulse/saw/triangle/noise
  waves with SID ADSR, pitch sweeps, PWM, hard sync, ring modulation and the SID filter (LP/BP/HP sweeps).
- They ship as small mono OGG files (22 kHz) in assets/sfx/ (~670 KB in total). The PC needs no extra tools.
  To rebuild them on a machine that has xa, sidplayfp and ffmpeg: node tools/sfx-build/buildSfx.js assets/sfx
- assets/sfx.js plays them in the browser through WebAudio as an OVERLAY. The room music (<audio id="room-audio">)
  keeps playing and is only ducked: to 70% while an effect sounds, 45% for death and level-up, gliding back in ~150 ms.

EVENT -> SOUND WIRING
- Footsteps: every ~0.85 tiles walked (polls playerPosX/Y), alternating step1/step2 with slight pitch variation.
- Room entry (currentDungeon.geoKey changes): room_enter (soft whoosh + low ring-mod bell); the ambience crossfades.
- Combat starts (dice_state with a new active 'combat' action): combat_start (tritone brass stab + war-drum roll).
- Each d20 roll result (dice_state.results): dice rattle.
- combat_log / realtime_combat_result lines:
  'hits X for N' or 'takes N damage' -> hit; 'critical' -> crit; 'misses/dodges/parries' -> miss;
  'is killed by / slain / dies / falls' -> monster_death; casts/spell -> spell, or fireball for fire/flame/lightning;
  heal/cure/restore -> heal; 'All monsters have been defeated' -> quest. Up to 4 sounds per message are
  sequenced 220 ms apart. Repeated lines (the server re-sends the last 3 lines) are deduped for 5 s.
- The PC's hit points (window.combatCharacters, type 'pc'): drop -> hurt; reaching 0 -> death (Mortacia's lament);
  a rise of more than 2 -> heal. Level increase -> levelup (classic SID fanfare).
- action_notice: item-taken -> pickup (coins for gold/coins/gems); puzzle-complete -> quest; defeat -> monster_death;
  pursuit -> combat_start; door/gate/lever/chest/unlock -> door; spells/heal by keyword; failure/blocked -> soft miss;
  anything else -> notice chirp. Local pickups (DungeonActionNotices.observeItems) -> pickup/coins.
- New narrative text in #chatlog: door/gate/hatch opening -> door; taking gold/treasure -> coins; picking up -> pickup;
  casting -> spell/fireball; healing -> heal; 'level up' -> levelup.
- Per-sound minimum gaps and an 8-voice cap stop a burst from becoming noise.

AMBIENCE
- Layers: wind, sandstorm, drips, fire, howls, chant, rumble, water (seamless 10-18 s loops, crossfaded at the seam).
- The music LLM call now also returns "ambience": [{tag, level}] (1-3 layers), with no extra API call.
  normalizeSidAmbience() in retortWithUserInput.js validates it, with a keyword fallback. It is stored on the room's
  arrangement (cached like the music), /music/commit forwards it in roomMusicReady, and sfx.js starts it.
  Rooms cached before this pass have no tags; the browser picks layers from keywords in the room text instead.
- Each room plays its layers at its own playback rate (0.9-1.1, hashed from the room key) and loop offset, so two
  "wind" rooms do not sound identical. The level is quiet under the music (layer gain 0.32 x level x Ambience slider);
  crossfades take 2.5-3 s.

VOLUME / MUTE
- A small note button sits at the bottom-left. It has Music, Effects and Ambience sliders and Mute all, saved in
  localStorage 'holodekAudioSettings'. Browsers keep audio locked until the first click or keypress; sfx.js unlocks it then.

RISKS / NOTES
- Text-triggered sounds rely on wording (combat log templates are stable; LLM narrative is best effort).
  If a sound fires on the wrong line, edit the regexes in sfx.js combatSounds()/narrativeSounds().
- Hurt/death come from combatCharacters HP; if the server reports HP in another field nothing plays (no error).
- The music element's volume is now controlled by sfx.js (it was always 1.0 before).
- OGG/Vorbis plays in Chrome, Edge and Firefox (and Safari 17+).

FIX 2026-10-12: VOLUME PANEL DID NOTHING TO THE MUSIC; AMBIENCE COULD FAIL TO START
- Root cause: live room music plays through index.html's <audio id="room-music" src="/sid/current_room.wav" autoplay>,
  which is reloaded by playRoomMusic() on the 'roomMusicUpdated' SSE (server log: "Music updated for room ...").
  sfx.js only controlled game.js's #room-audio, which is not used on that path, so the Music slider, Mute all and ducking
  had no effect.
- Second bug: the ambience loop offset used a signed shift (h >> 3), so in about half the rooms it went negative.
  AudioBufferSourceNode.start() then threw, no ambience played, and the Ambience slider seemed dead.
- Fix in sfx.js:
  - music volume, mute and ducking now apply to EVERY <audio>/<video> element;
  - they are re-applied every 250 ms, on play/loadstart (capture) and on DOM changes;
  - bus gains are set with cancelScheduledValues and setValueAtTime;
  - sliders listen to input and change, unlock the audio context and apply at once;
  - the panel stops pointer and key events from reaching game handlers, and its z-index was raised;
  - the offset now uses >>>;
  - HolodekSfx.state() added for debugging.
- retortWithUserInput.js: roomMusicUpdated now also carries arrangement.ambience; sfx.js handles that event.
- Backups: assets/sfx.js.bak-volfix, retort/retortWithUserInput.js.bak-volfix.

TRANSPORT 2026-10-12: PLAY/PAUSE, REWIND, FAST-FORWARD IN THE NOTE PANEL
- C64-styled bar (blue/violet, monospace) at the top of the panel: <<  play/pause  >>  elapsed / total.
  The line below shows "n/N Movement - Song title".
- Rewind: back 10 s, wrapping at the loop. Pressed within 3 s of a movement start, or twice within 1.2 s,
  it jumps to the previous movement start.
- Fast-forward: ahead 10 s, wrapping at the loop. Pressed twice within 1.2 s, it jumps to the next movement start
  (after the last movement it goes back to the first).
- Movements come from the room's song JSON (GET /get-room-music, plan.form + plan.bars, with 2-bar transitions)
  and are scaled to the real audio duration. If no JSON is available, only the time is shown.
- Acts on the music element that is really playing (#room-music, or game.js's #room-audio). Whichever started
  last is the active one, and any other playing music element is paused, so only one plays.
- A user pause is saved (localStorage 'holodekAudioSettings'.userPaused). New room music and page autoplay are paused
  at once until the user presses play. The loop stays on.
- Panel keys and clicks are stopped from reaching the game.
- Backup: assets/sfx.js.bak-transport. Previews: /workspace/holodek-patches/previews/music-transport*.png (box).

TWO-SLOT ROOM MUSIC 2026-10-12: THE NEXT SONG WAITS FOR THE NEXT ROOM
- Problem: each new room's song overwrote sid/current_room.wav and was announced at once, so the old song switched
  ~40 s before the room appeared. Server log: music ready 12:30:33Z, [DungeonDelivered] 12:31:12Z for the same room.
- New retort/musicSlots.js: songs render into sid/room_A.wav or sid/room_B.wav, always the slot the browser is NOT playing.
  The browser reports its slot with POST /music/now-playing (on each switch and every 10 s). GET /music/latest gives the
  newest song for page load. The old current_room.wav is deleted at server start, so at most two song WAVs exist.
  An unplayed pending song may be overwritten by a newer one; that is intended (only the latest room matters).
- retortWithUserInput.js writeLatestJsonAndRender() and server.js /music/commit use the slots. roomMusicUpdated
  now carries wav (the slot URL) plus ambience.
- game.js: after the dungeonLoaded handler has spawned the player and called renderDungeonView(), two animation frames
  later it calls HolodekSfx.roomShown(geoKey). That is the "room is on screen" signal. The roomMusicReady handlers no
  longer play immediately when sfx.js is present.
- index.html: #room-music no longer autoplays a fixed /sid/current_room.wav. sfx.js loads /music/latest instead, and the
  roomMusicUpdated handler defers to sfx.js.
- sfx.js queue:
  - a song for room X waits until roomShown(X), then crossfades over 1.5 s between #room-music and #room-audio;
  - the old element releases its file afterwards;
  - if the room is already shown when the render finishes, the song switches at once;
  - for rapid room changes only the newest announced song is kept, and a new switch during a crossfade waits for it;
  - if the user has paused, the new song is loaded but stays paused until Play.
- Backups: *.bak-slots (server.js, assets/game.js, assets/index.html, assets/sfx.js, retort/retortWithUserInput.js).

REVISITS 2026-10-12: A SAVED SONG IS RE-RENDERED EARLY, OR REUSED, WITH NO LLM CALL
- Before:
  - On a revisit, getOrGenerateRoomMusic() found the cached song in sharedState (in memory, no LLM) and rendered it
    into the free slot, but only after generateMissingRoomDetails() inside the turn.
  - Revisited rooms are drawn by switchDungeonForCoordinates() (from IndexedDB / get-room-dungeon), not by
    dungeonLoaded, so roomShown() never fired there and the queued song would have waited until another room arrived.
- Now:
  - server.js /update-state: when the client syncs a room's coordinates (in the logs this is about 5 s before the turn
    starts) and that room has a saved song that is not already in a slot, prerenderRoomSong() renders it into the free
    slot and announces it (roomMusicUpdated, prerendered:true).
  - musicSlots.js remembers which room each slot holds and keeps a stable URL per render. writeLatestJsonAndRender()
    reuses a slot that still holds the room ("slot-reuse", no render). Going back and forth between two rooms renders
    nothing, and the turn's later announcement repeats the same URL, which the browser ignores as a duplicate.
  - game.js switchDungeonForCoordinates() calls HolodekSfx.roomShown() after drawing, so revisits switch songs on arrival.
- Note: saved songs live in sharedState memory; after a server restart a revisited room composes a new song (LLM).
- Backups: *.bak-revisit.

AMBIENCE GAIN 2026-10-12
- Ambience sat about 20 dB under the music (layer 0.32 x level x slider 0.6). Now:
  - each layer gets a trim (AMB_TRIM) that brings it to about -28 dBFS mean; the music averages about -26;
  - layer gain = trim x level; the Ambience slider defaults to 1.0 and goes up to 2.0;
  - a typical 2-3 layer room now mixes to about -27 dB mean;
  - a limiter on the ambience bus (threshold -4 dB, ratio 20) prevents clipping at 2x;
  - saved settings get version 2, which resets the old quiet default (amb <= 1) to 1.0.
- Backup: assets/sfx.js.bak-ambgain.

KEY-AWARE PITCHED AMBIENCE 2026-10-12
- Three new SID-rendered stems, all in A (assets/sfx/, 97 KB total; build: node tools/sfx-build/keyedStems.js assets/sfx):
  - amb_drone_A.ogg: 16 s seamless loop, A2 saw + E3 pulse (tonic + fifth), A1 pedal, moving vowel band-pass;
  - amb_drip_A.ogg: one A5 drip;
  - amb_howl_A.ogg: one howl gliding up to A4.
- When the room's song JSON is known (GET /get-room-music):
  - chant becomes the drone, transposed to the current movement's tonic with playbackRate (at most 6 semitones);
  - drips and howls become scheduled single notes from the song's scale/mode (drips any degree; howls tonic, fifth or
    third) every 0.8-3.5 s and 7-16 s.
- The key per movement is parsed from plan.form ("Name [F minor, ..." / "transition -> G minor"), scaled to the real
  audio length, and read from the playing element's currentTime every 250 ms.
  - A normal key change (transition) glides the drone over 4 s; a rewind/fast-forward jump glides in 0.6 s.
  - Paused music holds the key.
- Noise layers are unchanged. Without song data the original amb_chant/drips/howls loops play as before.
- Checks on the box:

  - headless Chrome, drone rate: F minor 0.7937, G minor 0.8909, D dorian 1.3348 at the right movements, incl. a

== RHYTHMIC AMBIENCE (assets/sfx.js) ==
- While music is audible and playing, ambience follows the song's grid: one bar = audio duration / plan.bars (4/4, 16ths),
  synced to the music's currentTime (smoothed clock offset), scheduled 0.7 s ahead on the WebAudio clock.
  Handles seek/rewind/FF (scheduled-ahead notes cancelled), loop wrap, crossfade and new-room songs.
- Drips: offbeat/syncopated 16th patterns per movement (dense in Danger, sparse in Reflection), in key.
- Howls: downbeat of each phrase start (4 bars in Danger, 8 elsewhere). Chant drone: swell each bar (chord change).
- Continuous layers (wind/sandstorm/etc.): phrase-shaped gain + low-pass swells; intensity follows movement mood.
- Free mode (music paused, music slider ~0, Mute all, or no song data): random gusts and random event timing.
  Mode handoff is smooth: automation holds its current value and glides into the new mode (no jumps).
- Debug: HolodekSfx.rhythm() -> {mode, clkOff, evLog}.

== CHARACTER VOICES (assets/sfx.js, assets/sfx/vox_*.ogg, tools/sfx-build/voices.js) ==
- Wordless SID "voices" when characters speak. Trigger: the dialogue subtitle panel (#dungeon-dialogue-caption /
  #dungeon-dialogue-words, one per displayed speech part) and quoted "..." speech in the chat log when a non-PC
  actor's name (or first name) appears within ~80 chars of the quote.
- Type from the actor record (getDungeonLifeContext().actors: name, type, sprite.spec race/class/sex/level/traits):
  regal (Mortacia, Suzerain, king/lord/..., level>=18) > whisper (undead/spirit words, spectral body, translucent)
  > growl (beast words or beast head on a monster) > murmur (everyone else: syllable babble with vowel band-pass sweeps).
- Phrase length ~ quote length (0.35 s + 28 ms/char, cap 2 s); stable per-character pitch/rate/vowel from a name hash,
  female +4, large -4 semitones; phrase-final fall. Murmur/regal pitches snap to the movement's scale while music is audible.
- Effects slider / Mute all respected; music ducks to 0.75 while a voice speaks. Debug: HolodekSfx.voices(), voiceProfile(name).
- Rebuild clips: node tools/sfx-build/voices.js assets/sfx (box only; needs xa + sidplayfp + ffmpeg).

== CHARACTER VOICES v2 (assets/sfx.js) ==
- Replaces the clip-based babble: voices are synthesized live in WebAudio with SID-style parts (pulse/saw/tri oscillators
  on one pitch control, sub-octave rasp AM, noise breath, two resonant formant band-passes, 0.7 s room convolver tail).
  All envelopes/pitch/formant moves are setTargetAtTime glides (no clicks). vox_*.ogg from v1 are no longer used.
- Per-character suite from the actor data (sprite.spec race/class/sex/level/traits size/build/fangs/tusks/translucency/
  bodyPlan, personality/voice/history/motivation/job): base pitch, formant scale + vowel set, waveform mix, breathiness,
  rasp, vibrato depth/rate, speaking rate, pause length, intonation range, signature sounds (laugh/sigh/grunt/hiss).
- Long lines: every sentence voices its first ~8-11 words then trails off; breaths/pauses at commas and sentence ends;
  questions rise, '!' stresses, periods fall; up to ~14 s per displayed speech part; a new part from the same speaker
  fades out the previous one; max two talkers at once.
- Doom-style cries per character (pitch-varied per play): sight (combat start, hostile visible monsters), attack/pain
  (combat log "A hits B for N"), death ("B is killed by A"), attack on misses.
- Effects slider / Mute all respected; music ducks while speaking; humanoid/regal pitches snap to the movement's scale
  while music is audible. Debug: HolodekSfx.voices(), voiceProfile(name), voiceSound(name, 'pain'), renderVoiceDemo(items).

== STEREO (assets/sfx.js only) ==
- Master stereo stage: all WebAudio -> 2x2 matrix (Stereo width fold-down, Swap L/R) -> speakers.
- Listener = game.js globals playerPosX/playerPosY/playerAngle; actors at mazeX/renderMazeX + 0.5.
- Voices (babble) + cries pan by speaker bearing vs. facing, re-panned every 50 ms (smoothed), distance gain 1/(1+(d-1)/6) >= 0.35, behind x0.88. Unknown position -> centre.
- Hits/misses/crits/spells toward the target named in the combat log line; deaths toward the victim. Footsteps near centre (+-0.06 alternating). Doors/coins/pickups centre with slight random width.
- Ambience layers: stable per-room world bearing (wind left-back, drips/water right, fire, howls far + drifting), max ~0.6 pan, rotate with facing. chant/rumble/sand: mid/side widened.
- Music: <audio> elements routed via MediaElementSource once the context runs; mid/side widen (side = 13.7 ms delay, HP 320 Hz, 0.28*width). Mono sum exact. SID render untouched.
- Panel: Stereo width (0 = mono .. 1.5) and Swap L/R, saved in holodekAudioSettings (width, swapLR).
- Backups on PC: *.bak-stereo. Page refresh only.
